The Fine Print (FAQ)

Will 100 pushups make me strong?
Yes, but you'll hit a plateau quickly. Progressive overload (adding weight) is better than adding volume (doing more reps).
How do I unlock The Zone?
It's a real psychological state called 'Flow'. Professional athletes hit it when the challenge perfectly matches their skill level.
Can I learn the Kamehameha?
We are still waiting on peer-reviewed studies for Ki manipulation. Start with the pushups first.
