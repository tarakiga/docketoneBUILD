Sailing Through Hell

In 40k, FTL travel requires tearing a hole in reality and entering the Immaterium (The Warp). The Warp is a dimension of pure psychic energy... and it is inhabited by Daemons.

The Navigator's Burden

You cannot use a computer to navigate chaos. You need a Navigator, a mutant with a third eye who can see the psychic beacon of the Astronomican (The Emperor's Light) on Terra. They steer the ship through storms of madness.

Temporal Drift

Time does not exist in the Warp. * Realspace Trip: 1 year. * Warp Time: 2 weeks. * Arrival: You might arrive 100 years late. Or 10 years before you left. Or you might arrive... wrong.

The Fine Print (FAQ)

What is a Gellar Field?
A bubble of 'reality' around the ship. If it fails, the laws of physics stop working inside the ship, and Daemons manifest instantly.
Why do they do it?
It is the only way to hold a galaxy-spanning empire together. Without Warp travel, humanity is isolated and dies.
Is the Webway safer?
Yes, the Eldar use the Webway (tunnels between reality and warp). The Emperor tried to build one for humans... it didn't end well.
