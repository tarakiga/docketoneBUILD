The Skinner Box

Gacha games (Genshin Impact, FGO, etc.) are casinos dressed up as RPGs. They rely on Variable Ratio Reinforcement: You know a reward is coming, but you don't know *when*. This floods your brain with dopamine in anticipation of the "Gold Light."

The Math of Pity

Most games have a "Pity System" (e.g., guaranteed 5-star at 90 pulls). * Without Pity: Probability is a cruel mistress. You could pull 1,000 times and get nothing. * With Pity: It turns gambling into a purchase... just a very anxious, expensive purchase.

Whales vs. F2P

  • F2P (Free to Play): Pays with time (grinding).
  • Whale: Pays with money (thousands of dollars).
  • Leviathan: Pays with... we assume bank robbery proceeds.

The Fine Print (FAQ)

Is it rigged?
Legally, they must publish the rates (e.g., 0.6%). But algorithms can be opaque. Generally, math is just naturally brutal without pity systems.
How much is a 'spark'?
Usually 300 pulls. At $2.50 a pull, that's $750 for a jpeg of a waifu. Choose wisely.
Can I beat the odds?
No. The House always wins. The only winning move is to play for characters you love, not for 'meta' power.
