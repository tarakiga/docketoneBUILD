Calculations for the Fandom Frontier

Welcome to Geek Galaxy, your premier computational outpost for the sci-fi, fantasy, and tabletop roleplaying universes. True fandom doesn't stop at watching the movies or reading the books; it requires digging deep into the lore, arguing about the physics of imaginary worlds, and obsessing over the minute details of spaceships that don't actually exist. We built these calculators to answer the incredibly specific, highly debated questions that keep fans awake at night, using real-world physics mapped intelligently onto fictional continuities.

Speculative fiction is at its absolute best when it is grounded by internal consistency and theoretical realism. That's why we take the "geeky" intensely seriously. By applying rigorous mathematical frameworks to your favorite franchises, you can finally settle those heated Reddit debates with hard, verifiable algorithmic proof. We use the real standard model of physics, orbital mechanics, and statistical probabilities and inject them straight into the lore of the franchises you love.

Want to calculate the exact Delta-V and metric fuel tonnage required for an interstellar spaceship to break orbit? Need to understand the energy output of a lightsaber in joules, or run statistical probability charts on your Dungeons & Dragons dice rolls to absolutely maximize your critical hit damage? This is the place. Gear up, load your parameters, and start calculating the physics of the multiverse.
