Navigating Neurodivergence with Data

Welcome to the Brain Modes calculation hub, a specialized suite of tools designed precisely for understanding and validating the neurodivergent experience. Traditional productivity advice and time-management systems are often built by and for neurotypical brains, leaving those with ADHD, Autism, or executive dysfunction feeling like they are fundamentally broken. We believe you aren't broken; you just need a different set of metrics.

These calculators are engineered to translate subjective, overwhelming neurological experiences into objective, mathematically validated data points. When your brain is hyper-focused on sensory input, or when you are struggling with the compounding weight of "task paralysis", having a deterministic algorithm map out exactly what is happening inside your nervous system can be incredibly grounding. It removes the guilt from the equation and replaces it with actionable physics.

For example, you can calculate the exact decibel threshold that triggers auditory sensory overload, or quantify how many actual "spoons" (units of mental energy) you have burned through during daily social interactions. Understanding your personal neurological baselines allows you to advocate for yourself in the workplace, structure your environment for maximum peace, and finally stop fighting against your own brain. By applying algorithmic logic to neurodivergent realities, we turn invisible cognitive loads into tangible numbers that you can actually manage and optimize.
